Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your measurements, jobs and settings stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings (language, units, pipe sizes, paper, theme), the current pipe, the last values entered in each tool, your recent calculations, your jobs and their cut lists, the free tries of Pro tools you have used, and a few local counters that decide when the system rating prompt may be shown. This data stays on your device and we do not receive it. Deleting the app deletes it. If your device backs up apps (for example to iCloud), this data is part of that backup and is handled under your Apple account.

Templates, cut lists and text summaries are created on your device and are shared only where you choose to send them.

Purchases

The optional one-time Rootgap Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

To verify purchases and restore them on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider (processor). The RevenueCat SDK in the app creates a random, anonymous app user ID. Each time the app starts, also if you never buy anything, it asks RevenueCat whether Pro is unlocked; RevenueCat receives that ID, your purchase history (product, date, store receipt) and basic device and app information needed for verification. We do not link this data to your name or e-mail, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat processes it only on our behalf, under terms that require at least the protection described here, and keeps it for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Ratings

The app may ask iOS to show its standard rating prompt. Apple decides whether it is shown, and any rating you give is handled by Apple.

Children

Rootgap is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Legal basis and transfers

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: the controller is yusgoktech (contact below). The purchase data above is processed to provide the purchase you ask for and to restore it (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). RevenueCat stores it in the United States; transfers are covered by the safeguards described in RevenueCat's privacy policy, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing and to data portability, and you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For purchase records you can contact Apple. You can also ask us to request deletion of the anonymous RevenueCat record of your app installation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
