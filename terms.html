2. Calculation aid, not engineering advice

Rootgap is a calculation aid. Results depend on the values you enter, on the fitting and pipe dimensions you choose and on general geometry. Fittings from different makers can differ from the published tables, and a template is only true to size when it is printed at 100 %. Before cutting or welding:

  • check every result and every printed template yourself,
  • follow the drawing and the piping specification,
  • follow the instructions of your supervisor or the responsible engineer.

Dimension tables follow published values of ASME B36.10M, B36.19M, B16.9, B16.5, EN 10220 and EN 10253-2, each checked against at least two independent sources. ASME and EN are standards of their owners; Rootgap is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
